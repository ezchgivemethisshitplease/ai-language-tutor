# Current teaching method

The evidence and limits are explained in [PEDAGOGY.md](../PEDAGOGY.md). The operational contract is [agent-workflow.md](agent-workflow.md). These documents replace the original prompt-only leveling and fixed lexical-coverage rules.

1. Teach meaningful English tasks with Russian explanations and contrastive examples, fading support only as relevant observed performance permits. Explicit requests for Russian always take precedence. L1 hypotheses require observed errors; nationality is not a diagnosis.
2. Combine comprehensible input, communicative output, focused form practice and fluency work across sessions. Strand balance is a planning aid, not an exact universal 25% requirement. The deterministic planner balances recent domain observations; actual time-on-task is not inferred from question counts.
3. Check comprehension directly. There is no corpus/lemmatizer or complete measured vocabulary inventory here, so never promise “95–98% known words” from known_words.txt. Choose manageable passages, check gist/detail, and adapt to the learner's response. Vocabulary labels are curated project choices.
4. Require retrieval before revealing an answer. The learner supplies explicit FSRS grades; Again means forgotten, Hard means remembered with difficulty. Follow library learning steps and due times. Avoid mandatory endless immediate repetitions.
5. Use specific focused feedback: prompt self-correction when useful, explain the relevant form/meaning contrast, then ask for a fresh application. Distinguish alternatives, unnatural usage, register and contextual errors. Error feedback is not an automatic acoustic diagnosis.
6. Record actual exercises, answers, hints, dates, provenance and explicit diagnoses. Derive mastery separately. Repeated easy/assisted/AI-only successes do not unlock a unit. Revisit confirmed recurring errors and overdue mastered units using the planner.
7. Use an uncalibrated provisional skill profile for placement and periodic checks. The internal readiness gate requires distinct delayed evidence, unit mastery and all four core skills; real human review is required for course progression. No quiz percentage or simulation certifies CEFR or exam readiness.
8. Vocabulary prioritizes learner needs, current objectives, useful collocations/chunks and prior errors. Do not invent frequency ranks or known-word lists. Add only reviewed contextual cards; avoid duplicates. The 15-new-card/30-backlog settings are configurable-in-code product defaults, not scientific constants.
9. Practise perception with actual repeatable audio and production with actual speech evidence. Intelligibility is the goal. Accent, regional variation and identity are not errors. Browser/OS speech is optional; external voice reports remain unverified observations.
10. Keep the tone adult and concrete. Explain what the evidence shows, what is still uncertain and what to practise next. Never guilt the learner for inactivity or use a streak as a knowledge measure.
