# Learning-science evidence notes

The current educational policy and implementation boundaries are maintained in [`PEDAGOGY.md`](../../PEDAGOGY.md). This file replaces earlier research notes that treated provisional teaching ideas, vocabulary estimates and product defaults as universal facts. It makes no claim that this repository has demonstrated learning gains.

## Supported principles and qualified applications

- **Retrieval:** attempts to recall can improve later retention. [Karpicke & Roediger's vocabulary experiment](https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2008/2008_Karpicke_Roediger_Science.pdf) supports continued retrieval after initial success. It does not establish a single best card format or a guaranteed transfer to conversation. Cloze and bilingual recall can serve different goals; judge whether the cue elicits the intended meaning and form.
- **Spacing:** distributed practice can support retention; useful gaps depend on the material and the retention horizon. [Cepeda et al.'s synthesis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/) and [longer-term experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19439395/) do not imply that the latest possible review is always best. Card-level FSRS estimates and the unit-level review heuristic are different mechanisms.
- **Input, output and fluency:** [Nation's principles](https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/lals/resources/paul-nations-resources/paul-nations-publications/publications/documents/my-ideal-vocabulary-teaching-course.pdf) motivate varied communicative practice alongside deliberate form learning. Equal strands are a planning framework. Lexical familiarity helps select material, but [coverage estimates](https://openaccess.wgtn.ac.nz/articles/journal_contribution/How_large_a_vocabulary_is_needed_for_reading_and_listening_/12552221) depend on texts and word-family definitions. Check comprehension directly; no universal coverage percentage, vocabulary-to-CEFR conversion or fixed daily quota is enforced by the research.
- **Feedback:** [classroom oral-feedback research](https://kazuyasaito.net/SSLA2010.pdf) supports attention to errors and opportunities to repair. Effects vary by task, learner and measurement. It does not validate every LLM correction or justify treating a grammatical alternative as wrong. Focus feedback, ask for a repair and revisit the contrast in another context.
- **Contrastive explanation:** [McManus & Marsden's replication](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/studies-in-second-language-acquisition/article/online-and-offline-effects-of-l1-practice-in-l2-grammar-learning/CA0DB509C645F4BF815CC6A69F6251D0) shows why L1-related instruction merits careful investigation. Its English-to-French setting does not validate this Russian-to-English KB. An L1 pattern is a hypothesis to check against the learner's actual response, not proof of an individual difficulty.
- **Pronunciation:** [HVPT research](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/studies-in-second-language-acquisition/article/high-variability-phonetic-training-hvpt-a-metaanalysis-of-l2-perceptual-training-studies/6ABB8C1F32D88D53EA8D05A4565E76F6) studies perceptual training with talker/context variation and feedback. A single TTS voice or a written minimal-pair list does not reproduce that intervention. Production and perception require separate observations, and neither is measured by typed spelling.

## Product choices are not established constants

The current mastery evidence thresholds, review interval, workload caps and language-support transition are conservative engineering choices listed in `learning.POLICY` and returned plans. They are transparent and testable, but uncalibrated. Do not describe the learner's observed item-success interval as a confidence interval for CEFR ability.

Short explicit explanations, sentence mining, shadowing, repeated talks and mixed review are available teaching procedures. Their value depends on content, task demands and implementation. Activity rotation alone does not prove effective interleaving, and a completed activity is not evidence of retention. No quota of films, minutes, cards or corrections is a universal learning requirement.

## What this repository has not established

There is no learning-outcome trial, calibrated placement instrument, validated phoneme scorer, licensed frequency inventory or guaranteed hours-to-level conversion. FSI estimates for a different direction or language pair cannot simply be reversed. Do not infer hundreds of known words from a few placement responses or promise a discount on learning time from cognates.

Use [`russian.md`](../../languages/english/l1-notes/russian.md) and its structured KB for the primary Russian-speaking audience. The existing Spanish notes are teaching references, not prevalence estimates or a claim that every Spanish-speaking learner shares the same problems. Observed performance and learner goals take priority over either set of candidate contrasts.
