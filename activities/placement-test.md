# Activity: provisional placement and skill diagnostics

Use Russian onboarding for a Russian-speaking learner. Confirm goals, interests, available minutes and whether an external voice channel is usable. Initialize through `tools/tutor.py init`; never copy templates over existing learner files.

Use `tutor.py items --level A1 --limit 6` to inspect authored diagnostic tasks. For a learner who reports previous study, sample a stated higher level with the same command; this is a targeted probe, not automatic placement. Present one item at a time. Keep answer keys hidden until an independent attempt. Save exact exercise/response files and call `attempt` with a stable ID and the current session. Record hints and rubric provenance honestly.

Observe grammar and reading separately from writing and speaking. Use fresh tasks to distinguish lucky recognition from productive use. The bank has 30 original tasks across A1–C1 and lacks listening recordings; it cannot locate a reliable CEFR boundary. Do not stop after two probes and call the level established. If further tasks are needed, author and validate them against specific can-do goals. Real listening requires actual audio; speech requires actual speech. Use the external voice pass only when available, and preserve “not assessed” for missing modalities.

Call `assess --level LEVEL` and explain a provisional profile: observed successes/errors, sample size, uncertainty and missing skills. Working level defaults to A1; legacy levels remain unverified. Advanced entry without demonstrated prerequisites remains conservative and requires collecting/reviewing evidence; do not invent mastery to bypass it. The engine's readiness gate and human-reviewed progression govern course transitions. Neither generated exams nor a model impression certify CEFR.

Never generate hundreds of “known” words from a nominal level. Preserve old known_words as legacy data and add vocabulary evidence only when observed. Seed a small number of reviewed, useful contextual cards from actual mistakes or learner requests. Ask for genuine recall grades. Explain in Russian: «Это предварительный профиль. Мы уточним его по разным заданиям и отсроченным повторениям; непроверенные навыки пока неизвестны».
