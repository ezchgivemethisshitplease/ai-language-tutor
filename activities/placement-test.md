# Activity: provisional placement and skill diagnostics

Use Russian onboarding for a Russian-speaking learner. Confirm goals, interests, available minutes and whether an external voice channel is usable. Initialize through `tools/tutor.py init`; never copy templates over existing learner files. Save confirmed goals with `init --objective` or `configure --objective`.

Use `tutor.py items --level A1 --limit 6 --controller` to inspect original diagnostic tasks and save their full controller definitions. For previous study, sample a stated higher level with the same command. Present one learner view at a time: prompt, reading context, choices and success criteria, without keys or hidden audio scripts. Ordinary `items` redacts these fields. The bank contains 100 tasks, independently reviewed for declared meaning and alternatives; its levels are uncalibrated authoring targets.

Record each exact independent response before feedback through `attempt` with a stable ID and the current session. Mark hints, revealed answers and repeated families honestly. Unknown alternatives remain reviewable. Open tasks need contextual rubric review; AI ratings are `source: llm`. Later `review` adds an annotation to that same trial instead of changing the original or inventing another independent attempt. Never impersonate a human reviewer.

Reading and listening are separate. The 20 original listening contexts do not repeat the reading answers. `tutor.py audio diag-a1-listening-01 /existing/path/stimulus.aiff` renders local English stimulus audio on macOS without printing its script or playing it; Linux uses `.wav` with optional eSpeak. Actually play the audio before the answer. Store an audio reference and truthful `--presentation FILE` flags; do not show the transcript or key. Generating a file does not prove that sound was heard. Speaking requires actual learner speech; typed text stays unassessed. Use the external voice pass only when available.

Call `assess --level LEVEL` for the observed profile and `placement --target-level LEVEL` for a provisional advanced-entry report. Report successes, errors, sample size, uncertainty and missing production. `accept-placement` requires fresh independent authored reading/listening evidence across several units. It selects materials and records assumed prerequisites with confirmation probes; it leaves baseline proficiency and unit mastery unchanged. Writing and speaking remain separately unknown until assessed. The exact safeguards and audio evidence schema are in `docs/assessment.md`.

Diagnostic items never establish unit mastery. Actual course progression still requires every current-level unit, fresh broad reading/listening/writing/speaking evidence and a real human review. A retained provisional route can request rechecking without silently resetting the learner. Neither a model impression nor these diagnostics certify CEFR.

Never generate hundreds of known words from a nominal level. Preserve old lists as legacy data and add vocabulary evidence only when observed. Seed a small number of reviewed contextual cards from actual mistakes or learner requests. Ask for genuine recall grades. Explain: «Это предварительный профиль. Мы уточним его по разным заданиям и отсроченным повторениям; непроверенные навыки пока неизвестны».
